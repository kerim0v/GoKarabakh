# GoKarabakh 🏔️

## Tours, hotels, and local guides in Karabakh — all in one place.

GoKarabakh is a full-stack booking platform built as our final project at Holberton School. The region is opening up to travelers again, but there's no single place to plan a trip — you'd have to hunt through scattered social media pages and word-of-mouth recommendations just to book a guide or find a place to stay. GoKarabakh fixes that by bringing tours, accommodations, and local guides into one platform.

What it does
Browse and book tours across Karabakh — historical sites, nature routes, cultural experiences
Find and reserve hotels and other accommodations by location, dates, and price
Connect with local guides — see their languages, specialties, and availability, and book them directly
Manage bookings from a personal dashboard

# Planned / stretch features

We're also considering:

🗺️ Interactive map view of tours and points of interest\
🌍 Multi-language support (Azerbaijani, English, Russian)\

⭐ Reviews and ratings for guides, hotels, and tours\
📖 Short cultural/historical background on each site (a lot of visitors won't know the history — this adds context)\
